# A One-Cut Large-Sieve Proof of a Quasi-Riemann Zero-Free Region

Yuwen Wang
School of Mechanical Engineering, Xi'an Jiaotong University, Xi'an, China
Email: 3010317@proton.me

Preprint (expanded edition, 20 pages).

We present a simpler proof of the quasi-Riemann zero-free half-plane Re s > 47/48
for Dirichlet L-functions and finite-order Hecke L-functions over the Eisenstein
field. Exact Möbius inversion and a single cutoff separate a cubic completion
into two ranges; an existing theta estimate controls the small-divisor range,
while the sextic large sieve controls all remaining scales uniformly. This
replaces transfer and recursive descent in the dual mean-square argument.
The conductor-independent boundary also excludes Landau–Siegel zeros.
